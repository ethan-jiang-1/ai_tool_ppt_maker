# Style Master Prompt

```
Design a visual style guide for a keynote slide deck. This is a reference image, not a slide itself. The deck uses etching and sketch illustration (fine sepia ink, visible hand-drawn lines, cross-hatching) as its DRAWING TECHNIQUE — but the SUBJECTS it depicts are strictly CONTEMPORARY: present-day people (modern casual / business-casual clothing), current devices (laptops, phones, screens), and present-day workplaces. Historical technique, present-day subjects. No period costume, no 19th-century dress, no antique props.

Show clearly:

Color palette: 5 swatches with hex codes, labeled — cream paper #F5F0EB, warm panel #EDE5DA, sepia ink #2D1B11, faded ink #6B5B4F, amber wash #D97706.

Typography: headline sample (large serif, sepia ink), subtitle sample (medium, faded ink), body text sample (small, readable), pull-quote sample (large italic with amber quotation marks). Clear size hierarchy.

Layout grid: wireframe showing kicker zone (small caps + thin amber line), title zone, main content zone, bottom callout bar.

THREE SKETCH USAGE MODES — the most important part:

MODE 1 CENTERPIECE SKETCH: A large detailed etching filling 60% of a slide area. Example: a present-day workstation — a laptop with lines of code cascading out, or a modern open-plan office — drawn in fine sepia ink lines with cross-hatched shadows. The sketch IS the slide.

MODE 2 MARGINAL SKETCH: A small quick sketch (8-10% of slide) positioned beside a text block like a margin note in a notebook. Example: beside a pull-quote about AI agents, a tiny hand-drawn present-day knowledge-worker (hoodie, laptop) with autonomous motion lines.

MODE 3 DIAGRAMMATIC SKETCH: Abstract concepts as hand-drawn diagrams. Example: an information chain drawn as linked present-day office workers passing data bundles along a line, with sketched arrows and hand-lettered labels. Not polished vector — a thinking sketch.

Component examples (all in sketch style): One KPI card with oversized hand-drawn amber number and small label. One comparison layout with two columns, left muted, right with amber accent border, sketch illustrations in each. One timeline with hand-drawn horizontal line and small amber ink dots at year markers.

Micro decoration examples (tiny sketch mnemonics for abstract AI concepts, each no larger than 8% of frame, positioned as margin notes): Beside 'AI Agent' — tiny autonomous figure with motion lines. Beside 'Information Chain' — tiny linked figures passing bundles. Beside 'Harness Engineering' — tiny guardrails with cross-hatched texture. Beside 'Framed Autonomy' — tiny geometric frame with figure moving freely inside. Beside 'Organizational Pyramid' — tiny stacked figures forming a triangle.

Overall style: warm, human, intellectual. Cream paper, brown ink sketches, amber highlights — the etching technique of a field notebook applied to PRESENT-DAY scenes (modern people, laptops, current offices). Sketch lines are visible, slightly irregular, with cross-hatching for depth. Not cold vector graphics — warm hand-drawn marks on paper. Serif typography. This is for a strategic keynote about AI disrupting information work, for executives — not a tech demo.

No real company logo, no watermark, no page number. No photograph. No 3D render. No neon. No blue tones. No clip art. No smooth vector icons.
```
