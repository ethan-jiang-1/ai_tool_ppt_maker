# Deck System Contract — Warm Editorial
# Textual constraints. Visual constraints are in style_master.jpg.

LANGUAGE: English only. No Chinese characters on slides.

DECK TYPE: Brand story or editorial keynote. Every slide should feel crafted, not templated.

BACKGROUND: Warm cream #fef9ef on every slide. No white backgrounds (too clinical). No dark backgrounds (loses warmth).

COLOR FAMILY: Warm earth tones. #2d2d2d (warm charcoal) for primary text. #c44d34 (rust red) for emphasis and CTA. #b8860b (dark gold) for premium/accent. #7c9082 (sage green) for secondary. #f5f0e8 for panels.

FORBIDDEN:
- NO cold blues or corporate grays
- NO neon or fluorescent colors
- NO pure white backgrounds
- NO dark backgrounds
- NO stock photos or clip art
- NO real company logos
- NO watermarks or draft labels
- NO page numbers
- NO dashboard-style data displays
- NO dense tables or grids

TEXT DENSITY: Under 30 words per slide. This is editorial — white space is a design element. Max 2-3 body text zones.

HEADER LOCK: Do NOT render the slide kicker, title, or subtitle. The top y=0 to y=260 is a FIXED RESERVED HEADER BAND. Keep this zone clean cream. Python/Pillow will overlay the header text. For full-page slides (openers, closers), render complete slide with header text.

BODY TEXT CONTRACT: Body text should feel crafted — use pull-quotes, single-statements, and visual metaphors, not bullet points. KPI numbers are rare in this style — use them sparingly as dramatic visual anchors, not data displays. Max 2-3 distinct text zones.

CALLOUT BAR: Every content slide ends with an editorial takeaway at y=805 to y=900. One sentence, rust red (#c44d34) or muted gold. This is the "so what" for the human reader.

IMAGERY: Use abstract visual metaphors, not literal representations. Textured backgrounds (subtle grain or paper texture) are welcome. Photography-style compositions rather than diagram-style.

TONE: Warm, human, story-driven. Not corporate. Not academic. Every slide should feel like it was designed by a human who cares about craft.
