# Deck System Contract — Warm Editorial
# Renderer-neutral natural-language constraints. Structured values live in color_palette.json.

LANGUAGE: English by default; Hans is allowed only when required and bundled coverage verifies.
DECK TYPE: Brand story or editorial keynote. Every slide feels authored rather than templated.
BACKGROUND: Warm cream #fef9ef with #f5f0e8 panels. No pure white or dark background.
COLOR: #2d2d2d text, #c44d34 emphasis, #b8860b premium accent, #7c9082 secondary.
TEXT: Accurate quote/header/body text is compositor-owned. Under 30 words, max 3 body zones, generous whitespace, no dense table or dashboard.
COMPONENTS: Quote, image-text split, timeline, visual-focus, restrained texture through declared assets/patterns. No cold corporate palette, neon, stock art, logos, watermark, or page number.
TONE: Warm, human, sophisticated, editorial.
