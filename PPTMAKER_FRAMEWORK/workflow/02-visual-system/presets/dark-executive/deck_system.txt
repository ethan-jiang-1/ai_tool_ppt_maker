# Deck System Contract — Dark Executive
# Renderer-neutral natural-language constraints. Structured values live in color_palette.json.

LANGUAGE: English by default; Hans is allowed only when required and bundled coverage verifies.
DECK TYPE: Executive keynote. Every slide exposes a management implication.
BACKGROUND: Deep navy #0a1628 with #1e3a5f panels. Avoid warm or one-note monochrome pages.
COLOR: #06b6d4 positive, #3b82f6 emphasis, #0d9488 analysis, #f4f8fc text, #94a3b8 secondary.
TEXT: Accurate header/body/chart text is HTML-compositor-owned. Under 35 words, max 5 body zones, no paragraph, microtext, fake UI, or source citation.
COMPONENTS: KPI, flow, comparison, data, thin geometric icons, restrained callout. No stock art, logos, watermark, page number, or undeclared overlay.
TONE: Confident, precise, data-forward, executive.
