# Deck System Contract — Corporate Safe
# Textual constraints. Visual constraints are in style_master.jpg.

LANGUAGE: English only. No Chinese characters on slides.

DECK TYPE: Formal corporate presentation. Every slide should be defensible in a boardroom.

BACKGROUND: White #ffffff on every slide. Subtle off-white #f8fafc for panels. No dark or colored backgrounds.

COLOR FAMILY: Blue-gray corporate. #1e40af (corporate blue) for primary brand and emphasis. #0f172a (dark blue-black) for text. #475569 (slate gray) for secondary. #059669 (emerald green) for positive data. #dc2626 (red) ONLY for risk/warning — use sparingly.

FORBIDDEN:
- NO neon or fluorescent colors
- NO dark backgrounds
- NO gradient backgrounds
- NO stock photos or clip art
- NO real company logos (unless user provides)
- NO watermarks or draft labels
- NO page numbers (user can add in PPT if needed)
- NO decorative or playful elements
- NO excessive red (reserved for genuine risk)

TEXT DENSITY: Under 45 words per slide. Corporate audiences expect more detail — but still not paragraphs. Max 4-5 body text zones. Bullet points are acceptable in this style.

HEADER LOCK: Do NOT render the slide kicker, title, or subtitle. The top y=0 to y=260 is a FIXED RESERVED HEADER BAND. Keep this zone clean white. Stage 3 (Node @napi-rs/canvas) will overlay the header text. For full-page slides (dividers, title slide, closing), render complete slide with header text.

BODY TEXT CONTRACT: Clear hierarchy — headlines larger, supporting text readable. KPI numbers must be visually large (>=72px equivalent). Tables welcome (clean borders, alternating row colors). Charts welcome (simple bar/line, blue with emerald highlight). Use exact text from prompt.

CALLOUT BAR: Every content slide ends with an executive implication at y=805 to y=900. One sentence, slate gray (#475569). Answers "what action does this imply?"

KPI NUMBERS: Large (>=72px visual), bold, corporate blue (#1e40af) for neutral, emerald green (#059669) for positive, red (#dc2626) for negative. Max 4 KPI numbers per slide.

TABLES AND CHARTS: Clean, simple, professional. Blue (#1e40af) primary data series. Green (#059669) highlight. Gray (#e2e8f0) borders. Alternating row backgrounds (#ffffff / #f1f5f9). No 3D effects. No pie charts with more than 4 slices. No stacked bar charts with more than 3 series.

TONE: Professional, measured, evidence-based. Not salesy. Not dramatic. Every claim should feel like it could survive a CFO's scrutiny.
