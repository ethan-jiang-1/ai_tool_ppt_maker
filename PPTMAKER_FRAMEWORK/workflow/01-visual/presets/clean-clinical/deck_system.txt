# Deck System Contract — Clean Clinical
# Textual constraints. Visual constraints are in style_master.jpg.

LANGUAGE: English only. No Chinese characters on slides.

DECK TYPE: Data-driven report or clinical keynote. Every slide should communicate one clear finding or insight.

BACKGROUND: White #ffffff on every slide. Panels and cards use very light gray #f1f5f9. No dark backgrounds. No gradient backgrounds.

COLOR FAMILY: Slate-gray text family with teal/sky-blue data accents. #1e293b (dark slate) for primary text. #0d9488 (teal) for positive/growth data. #0284c7 (sky blue) for emphasis. #f1f5f9 (light gray) for panels/cards. #d97706 (amber) ONLY for risk/warning signals — not for decoration.

FORBIDDEN:
- NO dark backgrounds
- NO neon or fluorescent colors
- NO stock photos or clip art
- NO real company logos
- NO watermarks or draft labels
- NO page numbers
- NO source citations on slides
- NO dashboard microtext
- NO excessive amber/red (reserved for genuine warnings)

TEXT DENSITY: Under 40 words per slide total (excluding header). Max 4-5 body text zones.

HEADER LOCK: Do NOT render the slide kicker, title, or subtitle. The top y=0 to y=260 is a FIXED RESERVED HEADER BAND. Keep this zone clean white/light. Stage 3 (Node @napi-rs/canvas) will overlay the header text. For full-page slides (dividers, closers), render complete slide with header text.

BODY TEXT CONTRACT: Keep body words readable — no font below what looks like 16pt. KPI numbers must be visually large (>=72px equivalent). Max 5 distinct text zones. Use exact text from prompt.

CALLOUT BAR: Every content slide ends with a key takeaway callout at y=805 to y=900. One sentence, muted color (#64748b).

KPI NUMBERS: Large (>=72px visual), bold, teal (#0d9488) for positive, dark slate (#1e293b) for neutral. Max 3 KPI numbers per slide.

ICONS AND DIAGRAMS: Clean, minimal line icons. Simple bar charts and line graphs with teal/sky-blue data series. No 3D effects. No clip art.

TONE: Rational, evidence-based, clear. Not dramatic. Not corporate-jargon. Academic-but-readable — every claim has visible supporting data.
