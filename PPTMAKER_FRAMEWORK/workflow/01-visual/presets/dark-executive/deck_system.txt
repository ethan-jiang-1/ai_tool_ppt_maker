# Deck System Contract — Dark Executive
# This file is read by Stage 1 (stage1_build_inputs.mjs) and injected into every slide prompt.
# It defines TEXTUAL constraints (what the model must NOT do).
# The visual style master (style_master.jpg) defines VISUAL constraints (what the model SHOULD do).
# They are complementary: style master shows, deck_system.txt tells.

LANGUAGE: English only. No Chinese characters on slides.

DECK TYPE: Executive keynote. Every slide must have a management implication visible.

BACKGROUND: Deep navy #0a1628 on every slide. No white or light backgrounds. No gradient backgrounds that introduce warm tones.

COLOR FAMILY: Blue-cyan-teal family ONLY. #06b6d4 (cyan) for positive/growth. #3b82f6 (electric blue) for emphasis. #0d9488 (teal) for data/analysis. #1e3a5f (steel blue) for panels/cards.

FORBIDDEN:
- NO warm tones (no red, orange, yellow, gold, amber, coral, pink)
- NO stock photos or clip art
- NO real company logos
- NO watermarks or draft labels
- NO page numbers
- NO source citations on slides
- NO dashboard microtext (tiny unreadable labels)
- NO fake UI screenshots with Lorem Ipsum

TEXT DENSITY: Under 35 words per slide total (excluding header). Never paragraphs. Max 3-5 body text zones.

HEADER LOCK: Do NOT render the slide kicker, title, or subtitle. The top y=0 to y=260 is a FIXED RESERVED HEADER BAND. Keep this zone completely clean — dark empty space. Stage 3 (Node @napi-rs/canvas) will overlay the header text after generation. If the slide is a visual pause/transition page (full-page), ignore this rule — render the complete slide including header text.

BODY TEXT CONTRACT: Keep body words large, deliberate, and readable. No font size below what looks like 18pt. KPI numbers must be visually large (>=72px equivalent). No more than 3-5 distinct text zones on any slide. Use exact text from the prompt — do not reword or paraphrase.

CALLOUT BAR: Every content slide ends with a full-width management implication callout at y=805 to y=900. Text: one sentence, muted color (#94a3b8 or similar). This tells the executive "what this means for us."

KPI NUMBERS: Large (>=72px visual), bold, cyan (#06b6d4) for positive, teal (#0d9488) for analytical. Always paired with a short label. Never show more than 3 KPI numbers on one slide.

ICONS AND DIAGRAMS: Simple geometric shapes only. Thin borders (#1e3a5f or #3b82f6). No photorealistic icons. Flow diagrams use connected circles/rounded-rectangles with cyan/blue arrows.

BREAKS AND TRANSITIONS: Section dividers and closing slides are full-page (AI renders complete slide with header text). Use larger, more dramatic typography. These slides don't follow the 260px header zone rule.

TONE: Confident, precise, data-forward. Not casual. Not academic. Executive — every visual element earns its place on the slide.
